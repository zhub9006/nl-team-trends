## Data Quality Notes

1. **Ties**: Some sources include tie games (pre-2007) while others exclude them. The standard practice is to exclude ties from win % calculations.
2. **Franchise Relocations**: Data for relocated franchises (Brooklyn→LA, NY Giants→SF, Montreal→Washington) may be split or combined depending on source.
3. **Dead Ball Era**: Win-loss records from 1876-1916 are approximate due to inconsistent scoring and record-keeping practices.
4. **1994 Season**: No official standings or champions due to player's strike (August 12, 1994 onwards).
5. **2020 Season**: 60-game COVID-19 shortened season; records not directly comparable to 162-game seasons.
6. **Interleague Play**: Beginning in 1997 and expanding from 2023 (full interleague), AL vs NL W-L data should be considered separately.
